# Immunai

> Immunai is a New York-headquartered biotechnology company (founded December 2018) that maps the human immune system at single-cell resolution and applies machine learning to drug discovery and development. Its central asset is AMICA (Annotated Multiomic Immune Cell Atlas), a cell-level immune knowledge base built from single-cell multiomics — RNA transcriptome, surface proteome, TCR/BCR repertoire and spatial transcriptomics — harmonized across thousands of studies covering hundreds of diseases and immune cell types, paired with an ImmunoDynamics Engine used for target discovery, preclinical evaluation and clinical trial optimization.

## API surface

Immunai publishes **no public API** as of 2026-08-04. There is no developer portal, no API
documentation, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL/gRPC contract, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, no
`/.well-known/` discovery document, no first-party SDK or CLI in any public package registry, and no
GitHub organization. The company engages through direct partnerships with biopharmaceutical companies
and research institutions rather than a self-serve or programmatic product.

Two subdomains — `api.immunai.com` and `app.immunai.com` — do publish DNS records, but neither serves
Immunai: the first resets the TLS handshake before presenting a certificate, and the second presents a
certificate for an unrelated domain and then returns 403. They are stale records, not API hosts, and
should not be treated as endpoints. The only machine-readable surface on the marketing site is the
stock WordPress REST API at `www.immunai.com/wp-json`, including a WordPress `mcp` plugin namespace
that returns HTTP 401 to `tools/list`. That is CMS infrastructure, not an Immunai product API, and it is
deliberately not registered in this profile.

## Data protection

Immunai's privacy policy (last updated 10 February 2026) names GDPR, the California Privacy Rights Act,
the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, and asserts
participation in the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, its UK Extension, and the Swiss–U.S. Data Privacy
Framework. A Data Protection Officer and a Data Security Officer are named, both reachable at
privacy@immunai.com. No security certification (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI DSS, FedRAMP) is published,
and there is no trust center or vulnerability disclosure policy.
